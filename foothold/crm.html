What this log can and cannot see. It records each person who opens this tool, when, and the device and timezone their browser reports.
  • No IP address and no city. A published page never sees the visitor’s IP, and it cannot call a location service — so the timezone below is the only location signal available, and it is only a rough one.
  • Names, not email addresses. Your workspace does not release email addresses to pages, so people are identified by display name and account.
  • Not tamper-proof. This is an operational record of who used the tool, not a security audit trail. The real boundary is who you grant access to in the Share menu.
  • Timezone and device come from the browser and can be changed by the person using it.

Keep this record minimal. Store only what you need to help someone: how to reach them, what happened, and where the case has got to. Never record full card numbers, bank login details, passwords, one-time codes or identity document numbers — you do not need them to act, and a list of scam victims is exactly what a fraudster wants. Delete cases once they are closed and the retention period you have set has passed.
